# signal

Stateless detectors over the quotes of one market (or one player-prop line family). The engine owns lifecycle and persistence.

- **Arb**: legs are excluded when derived, line-only, suspended, from a degraded source, or (prediction markets only: share/cents/naira forms) quoting less than `minTouchLiquidity` at the touch. A missing liquidity figure does not exclude a leg. Quotes are split by `RegulationOnly` and each group is scanned on its own, so legs with different settle rules never mix. Inside a group, legs more than `maxLegSkew` older than the newest leg are dropped. The outcome set is taken from every quote in the market before any leg is dropped and must be offered in full by at least one source (this guards correct score and other many-outcome markets); each settle-rule group needs a surviving leg on every outcome, so a stale, thin or suspended leg kills the arb instead of shrinking a three-way market to two legs. Effective decimal is `1/prob` for books and `1/(ask + feePerShare)` for prediction markets. Stakes are `100/(d·S)`. Two-way lined markets (AH, OU, Asian totals) use push-aware payouts: `margin = 1 - 100/worstReturn`.
- **Every sport**: detectors only see one `canon.MarketKey`, and the key carries code, line, player and period, so a Q1 spread, a 1H total, a first-set (`S1`) or map-one (`M1`) winner are separate markets and never mix with the full game. QUALIFY and ML (which include extra time/OT, `RegulationOnly=false`) only pair with legs carrying the same rule. The push-aware path is chosen by `pricing.SettlesAgainstLine(code)`, so whole lines on SPREAD/TOTAL/SET_HCP/TOTAL_GAMES/MAP_HCP/TOTAL_MAPS and on every O/U prop skip the refund scenario, and quarter lines get a reduced margin. Two-way ML/QUALIFY and yes/no props use `1 - overround`. EV, moves and PrizePicks line gaps are code-agnostic and run on every prop of every sport.
- **Legs**: every leg carries the `marketKey` and `line` it was priced on. Arb, EV and move legs share the signal's market; a line-gap book leg carries that book's own main-line market key and line.
- **Dedupe keys**: `arb|<marketKey>|<side:label=source,...>` (a change of best book is a new signal), `ev|<marketKey>|<source>|<side:label>`, `line|<prizepicksMarketKey>|prizepicks|<consensusBookLine>`, `move|<marketKey>|<source>|<side:label>|up|down`.
- **EV**: `p_fair·d - 1 ≥ evMin` only where a consensus fair price exists, and only when both the fair probability and the quote's implied probability lie inside `pricing.evProbRange` (floor-priced favourites at 1.001 dump the whole margin onto the longshot; on the live board the 5–10% band carried a median EV of 12% vs 5.4% inside 10–90%, almost all O/U 0.5 unders, so the configured range is 0.10–0.90). Brands of one feed give one signal per outcome, from the brand with the best price (ties go to the lower source name).
- **Line gap** (`type=line`): each book's main line is the one whose over probability (fair if available) is closest to 0.5, and the consensus line is the lower median across books. `Margin` holds the absolute gap. Legs are the PrizePicks over leg followed by each book's main-line over leg, with that book's line in `Label`.
- **Move** (`type=move`): fires when implied probability moved by at least `MinMoveFraction` (5%, relative: `|p_now - p_then| / p_then`) against a point inside `telegram.moveWindow`. `Margin` holds that relative move as a fraction; notifier multiplies by 100 and compares it with each user's `/move` percentage, so `/move 10` means a 10% relative change in implied probability. Leg 0 is the earlier price and leg 1 is the current price; `fromPrice`/`toPrice` repeat them and `change` is the signed `p_now - p_then`. Moves are persisted without those fields, so the engine and the API rebuild them from the legs with `DescribeMove`. Noise rules:
  - a start point counts only if a later observation (strictly greater `observedAt`) replaced it, so two prices from one snapshot never form a move and a price overwritten within its own snapshot is never a start;
  - the current price must have been held for `pricing.minMoveHold` of engine time since it first appeared, so a move is emitted on the first rescan of that market after the hold;
  - brands of one feed are checked once per outcome (best price);
  - derived, degraded and over-`maxOverround` quotes never move.
